1. Gulab Jamun [INDIA 🇮🇳]

Description

Gulab Jamun is one of India's most popular traditional sweets. These soft, round sweets are deep-fried until golden and then soaked in a sweet, fragrant sugar syrup. They are commonly served during festivals, celebrations, and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Milk powder
  • All-purpose flour
  • Baking powder
  • Milk
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamon powder
  • Rose Water
  • Cooking oil or ghee for frying
  • Chopped pistachios or almonds

Instructions

  1. Mix milk powder, flour, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add a small amount of ghee and mix gently.
  3. Slowly add milk and make a soft dough.
  4. Allow the dough to rest for a few minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into small portions.
  6. Roll each portion gently into a smooth, round ball.
  7. Heat oil or ghee in a deep pan.
  8. Carefully fry the balls on low to medium heat until they become golden brown.
  9. Prepare the sugar syrup by boiling sugar and water together.
  10. Add cardamom powder and rose water to the syrup.
  11. Place the fried gulab jamun into the warm syrup.
  12. Let them soak for several hours so they become soft and flavorful.
  13. Garnish with chopped pistachios or almonds.
  14. Serve warm or chilled and enjoy this delicious Indian sweet!
