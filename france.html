6. Crêpes [France 🇫🇷]

Description

Crêpes are thin French pancakes that can be served with a variety of sweet or savory fillings. Sweet crêpes are often enjoyed with fruits, chocolate, sugar, honey, or cream, making them a beautiful and delicious dessert.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • vanilla extract
  • Salt
  • Fresh strawberries
  • Banana
  • Chocolate sauce
  • Honey

Instructions

  1. Add flour and salt to a mixing bowl.
  2. Add the eggs and mix well.
  3. Slowly add the milk while mixing.
  4. Add sugar and vanilla extract.
  5. Mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  6. Heat a pan and add a small amount of butter.
  7. Pour a thin layer of batter into the pan.
  8. Spread the batter evenly.
  9. Cook until the bottom becomes lightly golden.
  10. Flip the crêpe and cook the other side.
  11. Place the crêpe on a plate.
  12. Add sliced strawberries, bananas, chocolate sauce, or honey.
  13. Fold the crêpe neatly.
  14. Serve and enjoy this delicious French dessert!
