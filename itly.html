7.Margherita Pizza [Itly 🇮🇹]

Description

Margherita Pizza is a classic Italian pizza known for its simple and fresh ingredients. It is traditionally topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil. Its colorful toppings represent the colors of the Italian flag.

Ingredients

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper
  • Garlic

Instructions

  1. Prepare the pizza dough and allow it to rest.
  2. Roll the dough into a round shape.
  3. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  4. Add slices of mozzarella cheese.
  5. Preheat the oven.
  6. Place the pizza in the oven.
  7. Bake until the crust becomes golden and the cheese melts.
  8. Remove the pizza carefully from the oven.
  9. Sprinkle a little salt and black pepper.
  10. Drizzle some olive oil over the pizza.
  11. Add fresh basil leaves. / Add a few fresh basil leaves on top.
  12. Slice the pizza and enjoy this classic Italian dish!
