2. Chicken Teriyaki [Japan 🇯🇵]

Description

Chicken Teriyaki is a popular Japanese-inspired dish made with tender chicken covered in a glossy sauce. The sauce combines sweet and savory flavors, making the dish delicious and easy to enjoy with rice and vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Soy sauce
  • Honey or sugar
  • Garlic
  • Sesame oil
  • Cooking oil
  • Sesame seeds
  • Spring onions
  • Cooked rice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Clean and prepare the chicken pieces.
  2. Season the chicken lightly with salt and black pepper.
  3. Prepare the teriyaki sauce using soy sauce, honey, garlic, and ginger.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a pan.
  5. Place the chicken in the pan and cook until golden brown.
  6. Place the chicken in the pan and cook until golden brown.
  7. Pour the prepared teriyaki sauce over the chicken.
  8. Cook on low heat until the sauce becomes thick and glossy.
  9. Make sure the chicken is completely cooked.
  10. Sprinkle sesame seeds over the chicken.
  11. Garnish with chopped spring onions.
  12. Serve the chicken teriyaki with warm rice and enjoy this delicious Japanese dish!
