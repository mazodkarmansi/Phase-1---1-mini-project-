3. Chilli Paneer [China 🇨🇳]

Description

Chilli Paneer is a popular Indo-Chinese dish made with crispy paneer, colorful vegetables, and a spicy and flavorful sauce. It combines soft paneer with crunchy peppers and onions, making it a favorite among people who enjoy spicy food.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Capsicum
  • Onion
  • Spring onions
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Green chilies
  • Soy sauce
  • Chili sauce
  • Corn flour
  • All-purpose flour
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut the paneer into small cubes.
  2. Prepare a mixture of corn flour, flour, salt, and pepper.
  3. Coat the paneer pieces with the mixture.
  4. Fry the paneer until it becomes crispy and golden.
  5. Heat a little oil in another pan.
  6. Add chopped garlic, ginger, and green chilies.
  7. Add onions and capsicum and stir-fry them.
  8. Add soy sauce and chili sauce.
  9. Mix the sauce with the vegetables.
  10. Add the crispy paneer to the pan.
  11. Toss everything together until the paneer is well coated.
  12. Garnish with spring onions.
  13. Serve hot and enjoy this spicy and flavorful Chilli Paneer!
