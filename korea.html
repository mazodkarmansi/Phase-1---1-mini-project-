4. Tteokbokki [Korea 🇰🇷]

Description

Tteokbokki is one of South Korea's most popular street foods. It is made with soft and chewy rice cakes cooked in a spicy, slightly sweet Korean sauce. It is colorful, flavorful, and commonly enjoyed as a snack or light meal.

Ingredients

  • Korean rice cakes
  • Gochujang (Korean red chill paste)
  • Gochugaru (Korean chill flakes)
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Sugar
  • Spring onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Water
  • Boiled egg

Instructions

  1. Soak the rice cakes in warm water if they are hard.
  2. Prepare the sauce using gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, and garlic.
  3. Add water to a pan and bring it to a gentle boil.
  4. Add the prepared sauce to the water.
  5. Add the rice cakes to the pan.
  6. Cook the rice cakes over medium heat.
  7. Stir occasionally so that they do not stick to the pan.
  8. Continue cooking until the sauce becomes thick.
  9. Add chopped spring onions.
  10. Add a boiled egg if desired.
  11. Sprinkle sesame seeds on top.
  12. Serve hot and enjoy this spicy Korean street food!
