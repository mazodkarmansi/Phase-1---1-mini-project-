5. Mango Sticky Rice [Thailand 🇹🇭]

Description

Mango Sticky Rice is a popular Thai dessert made with sweet sticky rice, ripe mango, and creamy coconut sauce. The combination of soft rice, fresh mango, and coconut creates a delicious balance of sweet and creamy flavors.

Ingredients

  • Sticky rice
  • Ripe Mangoes
  • Coconut milk
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Corn flour
  • Sesame seeds
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Wash and soak the sticky rice for several hours.
  2. Steam the rice until it becomes soft and sticky.
  3. Heat coconut milk in a saucepan.
  4. Add sugar and a small amount of salt.
  5. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
  6. Pour some of the coconut mixture over the warm sticky rice.
  7. Mix gently and allow the rice to absorb the coconut flavor.
  8. Peel and slice the ripe mangoes.
  9. Prepare a slightly thick coconut sauce for topping.
  10. Place the sticky rice on a plate.
  11. Arrange the fresh mango slices beside the rice.
  12. Pour the coconut sauce over the rice.
  13. Sprinkle sesame seeds on top.
  14. Serve and enjoy this delicious Thai dessert!
