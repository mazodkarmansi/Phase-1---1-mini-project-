8.Churros [Mexico 🇲🇽]

Description

Churros are crispy, golden fried dough sticks that are popular in Mexico and many other countries. They are usually coated with sugar and cinnamon and are often served with chocolate sauce.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Water
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Cinnamon powder
  • Cooking oil
  • Vanilla extract
  • Chocolate sauce

Instructions

  1. Add water, butter, sugar, and salt to a saucepan.
  2. Heat the mixture until the butter melts.
  3. Add the flour and mix until a dough forms.
  4. Allow the dough to cool slightly.
  5. Add vanilla extract and mix well.
  6. Put the dough into a piping bag with a star-shaped nozzle.
  7. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan.
  8. Carefully pipe strips of dough into the hot oil.
  9. Fry the churros until they become golden and crispy.
  10. Remove them and place them on a paper towel.
  11. Mix sugar and cinnamon in a bowl.
  12. Coat the warm churros with the cinnamon sugar.
  13. Serve them with chocolate sauce.
  14. Enjoy your crispy and delicious Mexican churros!
